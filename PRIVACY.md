# Wissh Privacy Policy

Wissh does not collect any data.

The app connects directly from your device to the servers you configure.
There is no relay, no analytics, and no account. Server addresses,
usernames, passwords, and private keys are stored only on your device, in
the iOS Keychain and app storage. Terminal content, files you preview, and
files you upload travel only between your device and your server over SSH.

When a jump host is configured, Wissh creates an end-to-end SSH connection to
the target over an SSH forwarding channel. The target private key remains on
the device and is not copied to the jump host.

Crash reports and TestFlight feedback are handled by Apple under Apple's
own privacy terms and reach the developer in anonymized form.
